Biography

I am a qualified Secondary School Teacher of Mathematics and ICT based in Uganda, holding a Bachelor of Science with Education from Kumi University. My work centres on making both subjects less intimidating — Mathematics through patient, step-by-step reasoning, and ICT through genuinely hands-on practice rather than theory alone. This hub grew out of notes and past papers I was already preparing for my own classes, reorganised so any student with a phone or computer can reach them.

Qualifications

Qualification

Bachelor of Science with Education (BSCED) — Mathematics

Kumi University, Uganda. A degree combining strong subject mastery in Mathematics with professional training in Education, qualifying me to teach at secondary school level.

Specialisation

Secondary Mathematics (O-Level & A-Level) and ICT

Focus areas include algebra, geometry, statistics, and calculus for Mathematics, and computer applications, programming basics, and digital literacy for ICT.

Ongoing

Continuous professional development

Regularly updating teaching materials to match the current UCE and UACE syllabi, and exploring how digital tools can support classroom teaching.

Teaching experience

I have taught Mathematics and ICT across multiple secondary school levels, supporting students preparing for UCE and UACE examinations, and mentoring learners who need extra academic support outside the classroom.

Areas of specialisation

Mathematics

Algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics, and introductory calculus for O-Level and A-Level.

ICT

Computer fundamentals, productivity software, internet & digital citizenship, and practical exam preparation.

Vision & mission

Vision

A generation of Ugandan students who see Mathematics and ICT as tools they can use confidently, not subjects to fear.

Mission

To provide clear, accessible, and well-organised learning materials and direct support to every student who reaches out.
