Study guidance

A few habits that consistently help students improve in Mathematics and ICT:

  • Redo worked examples without looking, then check your steps.
  • Practice past papers under timed conditions, not just untimed.
  • For ICT, actually use the software — reading about Excel isn't the same as using it.
  • Ask specific questions early, rather than a general "I don't understand" the night before a test.
